## Field Progress

- Device optimization is becoming more automated, with ML, surrogate models, and inverse design increasingly used to search large parameter spaces efficiently.
- Perovskite stability studies are moving from bulk defect narratives to microscopic impurity, interface, and dynamic-structure mechanisms.
- Characterization methods are becoming more operando and model-aware, improving the extraction of ionic and carrier properties under realistic conditions.
- High-throughput materials discovery is broadening beyond absorbers to include charge-transport layers, transparent conductors, and functional interfaces.
- PV research is increasingly coupled to grid and policy analysis, reflecting the importance of system-level deployment alongside materials innovation.

## Open Questions

- Which microscopic impurities and interfacial motifs are most responsible for initiating perovskite degradation under real operating conditions?
- How can AI-assisted device optimization be made robust enough to transfer reliably from simulation to experimentally fabricated solar cells?
- What processing routes best preserve favorable conversion pathways in evaporated all-perovskite tandem architectures while controlling bandgap and defect formation?
- Can generalized rules for band alignment and surface termination be developed that remain predictive across broader perovskite chemistries and interlayers?
- How should operando measurements and modeling be combined to separate ionic, electronic, and transport-layer effects in transient and frequency-domain experiments?
- What are the limits of using LLMs and reinforcement learning in PV policy and adoption models while maintaining behavioral validity and policy trustworthiness?

## Outlook

The September 2026 literature suggests a field that is becoming more computationally integrated, with AI methods increasingly embedded at every stage from materials discovery to device engineering and deployment planning. For perovskites, the next step is likely to be tighter coupling between mechanistic insight and manufacturable process control, especially for interfaces, defects, and thermal-history effects. At the same time, PV research is broadening into system optimization, forecasting, and policy design, indicating that future progress will depend not only on improving cell efficiency but also on building reliable, data-driven pathways from materials to markets.
